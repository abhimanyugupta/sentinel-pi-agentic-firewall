    Scope and limits
    
      
    What this prototype has not validated
    
      
      
        
    • This deterministic replay runs no live model. A separate static supplement shows recorded local Gemma 3 evidence; neither page makes model requests.
      • 
        
    • No blind holdout, independent red-team adjudication, or evidence of performance on unseen traffic.
      • 
        
    • The caller supplies host-bound provenance context to the deterministic gate. This prototype does not cryptographically authenticate source identifiers or prove a model's causal reasoning.
      • 
        
    • The support task checks its ticket against the synthetic customer record, while the host-created task contract controls tool, customer, category, and call budget. The answer guard matches fixture phrases; it does not verify paraphrased claims semantically.
      • 
        
    • Impact on productivity, cost, incident rate, or customer outcomes has not been measured.
